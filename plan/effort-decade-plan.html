What each row does

Front · Bodyguard

Takes 40% of every hit aimed at the back row. Gets 50% more special charge from hits taken. Ability: Shield Wall, which also stops Gale's Gust and shelters the rows behind from Vex's fire.

Middle · Interrupt

Damage dealt during a wind-up fills a stagger meter shown on the warning bar. Fill it and the attack lands at half strength. Grimm's Ground Slam needs double and is stopped outright. Time your Big Swing to the warning.

Back · Support

Every 20 taps heals whoever needs it most by 2.5% and clears slime, spores and burns. Patch Up does the same, bigger. Healers decide the Rory and Vex fights.

The problem

Today's rules run out in year two

I simulated a five-person squad (one keen walker at 11k steps a day, three around 7k, one at 4.5k) for ten years under today's rules: real step income, Taxman, XP, shop prices, upgrades, the weekly step goal, and every Sunday raid fought in full. Six different squads, averaged.

Nothing left to buy

Everyone owns the best gear at +10 by the end of year two. After that the average player goes 466 weeks without a purchase.

Runaway Efforts

Lap rewards multiply by 5.5 each lap. By year ten each player is sitting on 5,080,844 Efforts with nothing to spend them on. Raids pay 24× what walking does.

A wall at lap three

Lap health doubles, and there's no gear above Mythic. The squad's best boss never moves after year two.

The plan

Eight changes that make it last

1 · An endless ladder

After Vex the ladder keeps going: bosses cycle, and each step adds 7% of Vex's health, 2% damage and 1.5% reward (lowered from 2.5% after the decade simulation showed raids drifting to twice what walking pays). Steps add a fixed amount rather than compounding, because gear power grows in a straight line with Efforts spent. That's why progress doesn't stall.

2 · Raids pay like walking

Base raid reward goes from 150 to 70 per player, and lap rewards from ×5.5 to the gentle per-step growth above. Raids end up paying about the same as walking all decade, so steps stay the heart of the game.

3 · Four new gear tiers

Each one gives 1.45× Mythic's stats, compounding. Every class gets a full set of each, so 30 new items per tier and 120 over the decade.

  • Relic: level 25, 15,000
  • Ascendant: level 35, 32,000
  • Celestial: level 48, 55,000
  • Eternal: level 62, 110,000

4 · Seasons of 13 weeks

40 seasons in ten years. Each season brings:

  • a stretch of the squad's journey map
  • 12 weekly story chapters and a finale
  • an affix that changes every boss
  • a three-piece set with a set bonus
  • a free reward track earned by steps

5 · The journey map

The squad's combined steps move a marker along real routes. Five people at about 7k steps each cover roughly 2,400 km a season. Season one is Land's End to John o' Groats. The whole decade is about 95,000 km, nearly two and a half times round the world, with a landmark unlocking each chapter.

6 · Something every day

A weekly rhythm:

  • Monday: a new chapter and three squad bounties
  • Every evening: the Taxman
  • Wednesday: an optional five-minute Midweek Hunt
  • Sunday: the raid lobby

On top of that, a Double Stride weekend each month and six yearly events, each with its own boss: New Year Rush, Spring Sprint, Solstice, Harvest, the Haunted Walk and Krampus.

7 · Long goals and sinks

The things that stay worth saving for:

  • The Den: a squad base with 10 rooms of 10 levels, each a small bonus, about 1.5M Efforts in all
  • Cosmetics: outfits, colours and emotes
  • One-raid potions
  • A bestiary of every boss and affix
  • Titles and belt ranks
  • A yearly Hall of Fame

8 · A new boss every year

One new model and signature move joins the ladder each year, so 16 bosses by year ten. Ideas:

  • The Mimic copies your last special
  • The Hydra has two health bars
  • The Clockwork attacks on a fixed beat you can learn
  • The Tide alternates rows
  • The Twins must die within 10s of each other
Season affixEffect on every boss that season

Twelve affixes cover the first three years. From year four two are combined each season, which gives 66 pairings, enough that no two seasons in the decade play alike.

Decided 28 October

Three decisions, settled

You chose to cut raid rewards to 70, keep the step goal as it is, and build the endless ladder, Relic tier and Season 1 together.

1. Cut raid rewards from 150 to 70 per player

This is the change everyone will notice: Sunday pays less. Without it Efforts pile up into the millions and the shop stops meaning anything by year two. With it, raids and walking pay about the same for the whole decade. I'd announce it with the first season so it arrives alongside new things to buy.

2. The step goal for squads that walk less

A squad averaging about 5,000 steps a day never makes the goal past Wisp (6,000 a day), so in the simulation it stayed on Brett, Rory and Wisp for ten years. That's the rule doing its job, but it may feel stuck. Options:

  • Keep it as is.
  • Cap the goal at 7,000 a day.
  • From lap two, set the goal 10% above the squad's own four-week average.

3. Which comes first

I'd build the endless ladder, economy changes and Season 1 together, because each needs the others: the ladder needs the new tiers, and the new tiers need the season sets in between. The visual overhaul can run alongside.

Every season

Content drops

A new chapter set, affix and gear set every 13 weeks. Once a year: a new boss and an event. Every couple of years: a new gear tier. Each drop goes through the same simulations before it ships.

How this was tested

The fights use the game's own rules file, the same one the app and server use, with bots tapping at human pace (3–5 taps a second).

  • Boss health was calibrated by binary search per boss.
  • Balance tables use 60–80 fights per cell with random squads of four different classes.
  • The decade runs simulate every day of steps, with winter and summer swings and day-to-day noise.
  • Every Taxman, shop decision and Sunday raid is played out, with the squad choosing its boss the way the lobby does: up after a win, down after two losses, avoiding bosses whose step goal it missed.
